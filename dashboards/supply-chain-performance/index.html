Key insights

  • Skincare drives revenue: $241.6K, or 42% of the total, ahead of haircare ($174.5K) and cosmetics ($161.5K).
  • Mumbai and Kolkata together bring in 48% of revenue. Delhi trails at 14%.
  • Supplier 1 is the strongest partner: highest revenue ($157.5K) and the lowest defect rate (1.80%).
  • Supplier 5 has the highest defect rate (2.67%), and Supplier 4 the highest manufacturing cost ($62.71 per unit).
  • Quality risk: only 23 of 100 products passed inspection. 36 failed and 41 are still pending.
  • Sea freight is cheapest: average logistics cost $418 vs $562 for air.

How it was built

  • Data: Supply Chain Analysis on Kaggle, profiled in Python.
  • Model: loaded into a Power BI push dataset through the REST API.
  • Design: custom teal/orange theme (theme.json), rounded cards and a dark sidebar.
  • Interactivity: tile slicers for product type, location, supplier and carrier. Charts cross-filter each other, tooltips show values, and one button clears all filters.
